Who we are

FernFlow is a social media management service run by Luzaan in the Garden Route, South Africa. You can reach us on WhatsApp at 060 703 2347.

What this website collects

Nothing is saved by this website. It has no sign-up, no cookies and no tracking or advertising tools. The form and the plan builder only prepare a WhatsApp message on your own device; nothing is sent to us unless you choose to send that message.

Like every website, our host (Cloudflare) handles basic technical details such as your IP address to deliver the pages safely, and the fonts load from Google Fonts.

What we do with your messages

When you message us, we use your name, business details and page link only to reply to you, do your free page check and provide the service you ask for. We don't sell your information or share it with anyone else.

Your pages

Clients give FernFlow access to their Facebook and Instagram pages through Meta Business Suite. We never ask for your password, and you can remove our access at any time.

Your rights

Under the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) you can ask what information we have about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Just send us a WhatsApp. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, you can contact the Information Regulator at inforegulator.org.za.
