    About

    Hi, I'm Luzaan.

    I run FernFlow from home in South Africa's Garden Route forest, and everything happens online, so your business can be anywhere. I already manage the Facebook and Instagram pages for AuroSmart, a smart home company in George, so I know what it takes to keep a small business page busy, week after week.

    I write in English and Afrikaans, I answer my WhatsApps, and I look after your page like it's my own. My aim is simple: you stay with FernFlow because it's working, not because you're tied in.

    My business isn't in South Africa. Can you still help?

    Yes. Everything is done online, through WhatsApp and Meta Business Suite, so your business can be anywhere. Prices are in South African rand, and your posts go out at the right times for your time zone.

    Do I give you my password?

    No. You add FernFlow to your page through Meta Business Suite, and you can remove access any time.

    Where do the photos come from?

    From you. Real photos of your business work best; send them on WhatsApp whenever you take them.

    Can I check posts before they go out?

    Yes. You get the month's posts in one message to approve or change, then you can forget about them.

    How soon will I see a difference?

    Steady posting builds over time, so give it two to three months. Your monthly report shows how things are moving from the first month.

    Can I stop or change packages?

    Any time, with one month's notice. Moving up to a bigger package can start the next month.
